Data Governance

Who owns, submits, validates, approves, consumes, and governs the evidence

Governance in DPMES is a role map. It clarifies accountability and keeps every metric connected to ownership, review, approval, and public-sector responsibility.

Data owner

Defines what the indicator means, who is accountable, and which institution is responsible for stewardship.

Data submitter

Provides the reporting value, metadata, evidence trail, and reporting comments on the defined schedule.

Validator

Checks consistency, completeness, methodology, and compliance before the data moves forward.

Approver

Confirms official acceptance so the value becomes part of institution, policy, and national performance views.

Consumer

Leadership, analysts, planners, partners, and oversight teams use the evidence for decisions and reporting.

Governance rules

  • Every metric has metadata, frequency, unit, and owner.
  • Every submission has a review and approval path.
  • Every revision remains visible through audit and traceability.

Governance outcomes

  • Leadership sees trusted performance instead of uncontrolled reporting.
  • Institutions understand role-based accountability.
  • Partners and technical teams can distinguish draft, reviewed, and approved evidence.

Time-Series Story

Why time matters in performance monitoring and how to interpret change

Time-series data tells the story of movement, not just status. DPMES uses time to show whether performance is stable, improving, deteriorating, seasonal, delayed, or accelerating against strategic expectations.

Illustrative trend

How patterns become executive insight

What time-series means here

It means every indicator is not just a value, but a sequence of evidence points shaped by reporting frequency and institutional process.

What leaders should look for

  • Trend direction against target
  • Volatility or sudden breaks
  • Seasonal effects and reporting cycles
  • Lag between intervention and visible result

Why it matters

Time-series evidence helps distinguish one-off reporting spikes from durable progress and supports earlier decision-making.
